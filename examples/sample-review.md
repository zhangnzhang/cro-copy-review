## Top 3 conversion killers

🚨 **Issue #1: No benefits at all**
Original: "a 2000W motor, 5 power settings, a HEPA 13 filter"
Why it hurts: These are features. The buyer doesn't care about 2000W; they care about how long cleaning takes and whether the air in their home is cleaner. This is the textbook "2000W motor → more time with your family" mistake.
Rewrite: "The whole house in [N] minutes — and a HEPA 13 filter that keeps dust and pollen out of the air you breathe."

🚨 **Issue #2: The CTA doesn't say what happens next**
Original: "Buy now!"
Why it hurts: In VWO's published Veeam test, swapping a vague CTA ("Request a quote") for a specific one ("Request pricing") lifted click-through 161%. "Buy now" tells the reader nothing about price, delivery or risk.
Rewrite: "Add to cart — ships today, [N]-day free returns"

🚨 **Issue #3: No social proof**
Original: (the whole paragraph)
Why it hurts: Social proof is one of the strongest trust levers — statistics, examples or quotes. A single line can do the work of a paragraph.
Rewrite: add one line — "Rated [real rating] by [real number] owners" or a short [verified customer quote].

## Full rewrite

> **The whole house in [N] minutes. Your weekend back.**
> A HEPA 13 filter keeps dust and pollen out of your air, and no cord means living room to upstairs without hunting for an outlet.
> Rated [real rating] by [real number] owners.
> [ Add to cart — ships today ]

*Bracketed values must be filled with real data. Never invent ratings, counts or quotes.*

## Next steps

1. **A/B test the headline:** A "The whole house in [N] minutes" (specific) vs B "Vacuuming, finally off your weekend to-do list" (emotional).
2. **Fix trust first.** Trust & Social Proof scored 0 — one real rating line will likely move more than polishing the headline.
3. **On mobile,** split the copy into three swipeable cards — time saved, cleaner air, social proof — one emotion per card.
