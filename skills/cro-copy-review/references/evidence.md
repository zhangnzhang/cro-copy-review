# Evidence library

Research findings and published tests to back up review comments. Quoting a specific study ("NN/g has found for 20+ years that most users skim") beats a generic "best practice says".

## How to cite

- **Name the source** every time: "In VWO's Veeam case study…", "Copyblogger reports…".
- **Say what was measured.** A lift in clicks is not a lift in sales. A B2B test is not proof for a $20 impulse buy.
- **Never present a finding as a forecast** for the user's site. Use it to explain *why* a change is worth testing.
- **Respect the tiers below.** Tier C figures are labeled as reported, not verified.

**Tiers**
- **A — checked against the original public source.**
- **B — published source linked; widely cited.** Treat as credible, but check the link before using it in anything public.
- **C — reported by a practitioner; original page not located.** Use only with the word "reported", or leave it out.

## Headlines and clarity

| Finding | Tier | Source |
|---|---|---|
| "On the average, five times as many people read the headline as read the body copy." | B | David Ogilvy, *Confessions of an Advertising Man* (1963) |
| 80% of readers never get past the headline. | B | [Copyblogger — Magnetic headlines](http://www.copyblogger.com/magnetic-headlines/) |
| Over 20+ years of eye-tracking and usability research, most users scan web pages rather than read word by word. | B | [Nielsen Norman Group — How people read online](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/how-people-read-online/) |
| When offered several headline styles, 36% of people preferred one starting with a number. | B | [CXL — 5 characteristics of high-converting headlines](http://conversionxl.com/5-characteristics-high-converting-headlines/) |
| Conductor's headline study: number-led and reader-addressing headlines resonated most; question headlines least. 51% preferred headlines with 0–1 superlatives. Most preferred sentence case, though about 1 in 5 preferred all caps. | B | [Moz — 5 data insights into the headlines readers click](https://moz.com/blog/5-data-insights-into-the-headlines-readers-click) |
| Clarifying that a product was a supplement increased sales 89%. | C | Cited in [Search Engine Watch — 11 quick UX tips](https://www.searchenginewatch.com/2016/07/14/11-quick-ux-tips-to-improve-landing-page-conversion/) |

**Use for:** Hook & Headline, Framework Application ("most people only read the headline, so it must carry the offer").

## CTA wording

| Finding | Tier | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Veeam changed a product-page link from "Request a quote" to "Request pricing". Click-through to the sales inquiry page went from 0.54% to 1.40% (+161.66%). | **A** | [VWO case study (PDF)](https://static.wingify.com/vwo/uploads/2019/06/veeam-software-increased-conversions_2019-06-29-19-30-54.pdf) |
| Changing "your" to "my" in a CTA ("Start your free trial" → "Start my free trial") increased conversions 90%. | B | Unbounce test, cited in [KlientBoost — Landing page copy](https://www.klientboost.com/landing-pages/landing-page-copy/) |
| "Try for 30 days!" outperformed "Learn more" by 18%; "Buy now" outperformed "Learn more" by 2%. | B | [KlientBoost — eCommerce landing page examples](https://www.klientboost.com/landing-pages/ecommerce-landing-page/) |
| About 90% of visitors who read a headline also read the CTA. | B | Unbounce, cited by [Constant Content — CTA tips](https://www.constant-content.com/content-writing-service/2018/08/cta-tips/) |
| ALL CAPS text is harder to read because words lose their shape — also an accessibility problem for readers with dyslexia. | B | [UX Movement — All caps is hard for users to read](https://uxmovement.com/content/all-caps-hard-for-users-to-read/) |
| Sentence-case buttons read as friendlier than Title Case or all caps. | B | [Making a case for letter case](https://medium.com/@jsaito/making-a-case-for-letter-case-19d09f653c98) |

**Use for:** CTA Clarity, Rhythm & Readability.

## Specificity and benefits

| Finding | Tier | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Apple launched the iPod as "1,000 songs in your pocket" while competitors advertised storage in gigabytes — the canonical feature-to-benefit translation. | B | Apple iPod launch, 2001 |
| ClickFunnels doesn't say you build a funnel "fast"; it says "in under 10 minutes". | B | ClickFunnels homepage copy (a widely used example of concrete vs vague claims) |
| An online store (BlendJet) reported about $163.6K in sales in 30 days from a one-click 10%-off exit offer shown to shoppers leaving the cart. *(The article gives $163,633.50 in the headline and $163,644.50 in the body.)* | **A** | [BDOW! (formerly Sumo) case study](https://bdow.com/stories/shopify-sales/) |

**Use for:** Specificity, Benefit > Feature. The BlendJet case is also a good example of *how specific numbers make a claim persuasive*: "discounts work" vs "$163K in 30 days".

## Reviews and trust

| Finding | Tier | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Shoppers who see a product page with 1+ reviews convert 52.2% more than those who see none; 11–30 reviews more than doubles conversion; 101+ reviews lifts it over 250%. | B | [PowerReviews — Impact of review volume on conversion](https://www.powerreviews.com/review-volume-conversion-impact/) |
| Products with at least 5 reviews are nearly 4× more likely to be purchased than products with none. | B | Spiegel Research Center (Northwestern University), *How Online Reviews Influence Sales* |
| Purchase likelihood peaks at an average rating of 4.2–4.5 stars, not 5.0 — a perfect score looks too good to be true. | B | [Northwestern University / Spiegel Research Center](https://news.northwestern.edu/stories/2016/01/opinion-techcrunch-reviews) |
| 91% of 18–34-year-olds trust online reviews as much as personal recommendations. | B | [BrightLocal — Local Consumer Review Survey](https://www.brightlocal.com/learn/local-consumer-review-survey) |
| Roughly 1 in 5 shoppers (18–19% depending on the survey year) have abandoned an order because they didn't trust the site with their card details. | B | Baymard Institute — checkout abandonment research |
| 75% of people judge a company's credibility by its website design. | B | Stanford Web Credibility Research, cited by [Kinesis](https://www.kinesisinc.com/the-truth-about-web-design/) |

**Use for:** Trust & Social Proof. These explain *why* a missing review count or rating hurts — but in the rewrite, still use placeholders (`[real review count]`) and never invent the numbers.

## Not included on purpose

Figures that circulate widely but whose original wording or method doesn't hold up (for example a claim that multiple offers "plummet conversion rates by 266%" — a decrease can't exceed 100%) are left out. If you add a finding, add its source and tier.
