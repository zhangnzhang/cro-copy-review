# Quick-scan checklist (30 checks)

Use when the user wants "just the main problems" rather than a full scored review. Run all 30, then list only the ❌ items.

---

## A. Hook & Headline (5)

- [ ] Answers "is this for me?" within 3 seconds?
- [ ] Headline is short (roughly 6–12 words)?
- [ ] Subheadline adds something new instead of repeating the headline?
- [ ] Has a concrete hook (number, question, promise)?
- [ ] Avoids the brand-name headline trap ("What is X?")?

## B. Benefit > Feature (4)

- [ ] Every feature translated into what the reader gets?
- [ ] No empty benefits ("high quality", "professional", "innovative")?
- [ ] Vitamin vs painkiller positioning matches the product?
- [ ] No raw spec lists in the selling copy?

## C. Emotional Resonance (3)

- [ ] Mobile copy commits to **one** emotion (caring, novelty, exclusivity, reassurance, pain, urgency)?
- [ ] Emotional words rather than rational ones?
- [ ] Word choice matches how the product feels?

## D. Specificity (3)

- [ ] Specific numbers, $ or %?
- [ ] Big numbers rounded ($2.6M, not $2,632,024)?
- [ ] No empty adjectives ("fast", "great", "premium")?

## E. Voice of Customer (2)

- [ ] Lines echo how real customers talk in reviews?
- [ ] No jargon ("empower", "leverage", "best-in-class", "solutions")?

## F. Rhythm & Readability (4)

- [ ] Sentence lengths vary?
- [ ] Sentence case for buttons and body, not Title Case or CAPS?
- [ ] Active voice?
- [ ] Skimmable on a phone (short paragraphs, bullets)?

## G. Trust & Social Proof (3)

- [ ] At least one type of social proof (statistics, examples, quotes)?
- [ ] Risk reversal (guarantee, refund, trial)?
- [ ] No fake scarcity (fake countdown, fake "only N left")?

## H. CTA Clarity (3)

- [ ] Verb-led?
- [ ] Says what happens after the click?
- [ ] No bare "Submit", "Click here", "Learn more"?

## I. Framework Application (2)

- [ ] A recognizable structure (PAS, AIDA, catchy intro, hero → benefits → proof → CTA)?
- [ ] Every information sentence passes "So what?"?

## J. Comparison & Metaphor (1)

- [ ] (New categories / abstract services only) an "It's like X, but for Y" line or a sharp analogy?

---

## Output

No score, no full rewrite, no next steps — that's the full review's job. Speed is the point.

```
Quick scan: X issues found

🚫 Headline (2)
• No concrete hook — add a number or a question
• Subheadline repeats the headline — use it to add new information

🚫 Benefits (3)
• "2000W motor" is a feature → "the whole house in 12 minutes"
• "Premium materials" is empty → name the material and what it does for the reader
• "Smart design" says nothing → "it learns the room; you don't have to"

🚫 CTA (1)
• "Buy now" is bare → "Add to cart — ships today"

🚫 Trust (1)
• No social proof at all → add [real customer count] or [real rating + review count]
```
